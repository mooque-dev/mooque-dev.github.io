Keela Contacts: A Record That Says What to Do Next

For a small nonprofit, the contact record is the CRM. The old page showed everything we knew about a donor. I redesigned it to show what changed, and what to do about it.

Role
Product Design Lead
Company
Keela

Tools
Figma, Untitled UI
2core screens redesigned: the contact record and the contact list
7record tabs scoped to the person instead of the database
1principle: show what changed before what is true
Stakes
A fundraiser decides who to call from this page. If they can't see in seconds that a donor's giving just jumped, the call doesn't happen, and neither does the next gift.
Tension
Fundraisers wanted the full history. They also had seconds between calls to decide what to do.
Easy path
Keep adding panels: lifetime totals, an insights box, a recommendations box. The old page had all three, with the same paragraph in two of them.
The call
I decided to organize the record around the person, not the database: tabs that belong to the contact, recent change before lifetime totals, and two clear actions.

What I would still refine

Owning the gaps in my own design is part of the work.

In my designNext step
1Template copy and casing. "Track, Manage And Forecast Your Customers And Orders" is e-commerce kit copy in Title Case, and emails were Title Cased too.Write for fundraisers, and never restyle data. Fixed in the demo.
2Red for a volunteering dip. A 10% drop in hours shown in alarm red reads as failure.Neutral color with seasonal context: the same month last year.
3"Interactions" lists people. The tab shows names, emails and phones.A timeline of touches: calls, emails, gifts and events.
4A database ID in Highlights. Noise for a fundraiser.Move it to a Details drawer for the admins who need it.

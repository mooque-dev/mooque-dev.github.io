Four rules I keep coming back to

  1. 01

    Show the work, not just the answer.

    On Aplos × Keela I showed every data mapping and the sync status instead of hiding them behind defaults, because finance teams had been burned by silent syncs.

    Read the case study: Aplos × Keela
  2. 02

    Surface errors while they are cheap.

    On Transaction Workflows, validation runs continuously, so a miscategorized transaction shows up now instead of at the final report.

    Read the case study: Transaction Workflows
  3. 03

    Design the decision, not the default.

    A 75% to 82% win hid a leak after donors clicked Edit. I argued to pause, redesigned that moment, and the winner reached 92% with conversion steady.

    Read the case study: Fee Opt-In
  4. 04

    Build confidence before capability.

    The top fear was emailing all 5,000 donors by accident, so a preview of exactly who a workflow will touch comes before anything goes live.

    Read the case study: Automation

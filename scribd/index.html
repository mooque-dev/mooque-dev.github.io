What the role asks, and where I’ve done it

Tests, including the ones that lose
On Fee Opt-In I paused a rollout that looked like a win, tested three new versions, and the slider took fee opt-in from 75% to 92%. The buttons and the dropdown lost, and the case covers why.
Plans and pricing
Automation went from a product area that didn’t exist to a paid add-on in about four months. 30% of 355 organizations used it, and the product’s ARR grew 13%.
Lifecycle
Automation is lifecycle email for nonprofits: welcome, thank-you and follow-up journeys staff build without code, with a preview of exactly who each one will reach before it goes live.
Onboarding and setup
The first thing customers of two merged products set up together was the Aplos and Keela sync. I made every data mapping visible before anything moved, so setting it up felt safe.
Mobile, and AI every day
ARND runs on iOS and the mobile browser, built with AI coding tools: 160+ merged pull requests. The musical’s tools ran on the cast’s phones every day for ten months.

How I find books

Through people I trust, the feeds that know me, and, honestly, the covers in a bookshop. I read fiction, self-development, history and autobiography. The one I keep thinking about lately is Demian, for how it holds good and bad side by side, and what it asks about philosophy and faith.

I think reading starts as influence: someone you admire, or a topic you’re already curious about. Then it becomes a habit, and sometimes a passion. The beautiful part is living, for a few hundred pages, a life you’d never get to live yourself. The risk is reading alone. Without talking a book through, it’s easy to keep only the parts that agree with you. That’s why a library and a book club in the same place makes sense to me.

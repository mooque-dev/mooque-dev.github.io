One payment, one receipt: before and after the split

From my Transaction Logging Redesign at Keela, part of Aplos (now Velora). The same gala payment, as the receipt looked before the redesign and after it, under three countries' rules.

The payment:

Before: one type per payment

The product made you pick one type, so there were two ways to log this. Neither gave one correct receipt.

After: one payment, split by type

Each part keeps its own type, so one receipt can show the whole payment.

Split Payment Receipts

One payment, three countries, one correct receipt

A supporter pays $200 at a gala: half for a ticket, half as a gift. Tax rules treat the two halves differently, and the product could only record a payment as one thing. This is the receipt before and after my redesign at Keela, part of Aplos (now Velora).

Ticket: she got something back, so it isn't a giftGift: the part that earns tax relief

    How I resolved it

    1. One record, split by typeEach part keeps its own type, so the product can tell the ticket from the gift.
    2. The total checks itselfThe parts must add up to what was paid before you can continue.
    3. One split, each country's wordingThe same split fills in the document each country expects. Legal and compliance set the required fields. My job was to make getting them right the easy path.
    4. The receipt shows before you savePeople doing the books see what the donor will get while they can still fix it.

<h3>Mentoring, Not Just Delivering</h3>
<p>I work with two designers on branding direction, storytelling, and engagement. My role is to give them ownership of decisions while keeping the work coherent. This is closer to design management than design execution. That's intentional.</p>
<p>Each artist collaboration starts with long conversations about their practice, inspirations, and audience. I treat every engagement as a mini brand strategy project: understand what the artist is actually trying to say before I design anything.</p>
<h3>Systems, Not One-Offs</h3>
<p>Rather than designing individual assets, I build flexible visual systems for each artist: typography pairings, color palettes, layout templates, and production-ready files they can modify themselves. The goal is to build capability, not dependency.</p>
<p>This philosophy is the same one behind Orchid. The best systems are the ones people can extend without you.</p>

<h3>Designing Within Real Constraints</h3>
<p>Every design accounts for manufacturing reality: printing methods, material costs, minimum order quantities. Artists operating on tight budgets can't afford a beautiful design that's impractical to produce.</p>

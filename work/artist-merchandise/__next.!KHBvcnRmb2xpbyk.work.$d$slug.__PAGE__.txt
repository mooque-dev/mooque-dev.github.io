<h3>Mentoring two designers</h3>
<p>I work with two designers on branding direction, storytelling, and engagement. My role is to give them ownership of decisions while keeping the work coherent. By choice, it's mostly design management.</p>
<p>Each artist collaboration starts with long conversations about their practice, inspirations, and audience. I treat every engagement as a mini brand strategy project: understand what the artist is actually trying to say before I design anything.</p>
<h3>A visual system for each artist</h3>
<p>I build a flexible visual system for each artist: typography pairings, color palettes, layout templates, and production-ready files they can modify themselves.</p>
<p>It's the same approach I took with Orchid: a system people can extend without me.</p>

<h3>Designing for production costs</h3>
<p>Every design accounts for manufacturing reality: printing methods, material costs, minimum order quantities. Artists operating on tight budgets can't afford a beautiful design that's impractical to produce.</p>

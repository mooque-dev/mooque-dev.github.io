Professional Work

Integration

Aplos × Keela: Connecting CRM and Accounting for Nonprofits

The first cross-product integration after an acquisition: syncing nonprofit CRM data with accounting and tax workflows across two codebases and two teams.

Feature Redesign

Transaction Workflows: The Feature That Was Causing Churn

Nonprofit accountants were spending weeks on tax reconciliation because the product treated every transaction the same way. A redesign that turned our #1 churn driver into a growth lever.

Design System

Orchid: A Design System Born From a Merger

Three products, 340+ scattered components, and three teams who didn't choose to work together. Orchid is the shared design language that made convergence possible.

New Feature

Automation for Nonprofits: When the Real Obstacle Is Fear

Nonprofit teams are chronically under-resourced. We built a no-code automation system from scratch, and the hardest design problem turned out to be fear, not complexity.

Experimentation

Fee Opt-In: Winning the Moment Donors Change Their Mind

A behavioral A/B test on Raisely's donation flow. The default was already working. The real opportunity was redesigning what happens the moment a donor decides to edit it. The winning variant moved fee opt-in from 75% to 92%.

Data Visualization

Cancer Care Dashboards, Revisited

Dashboards for clinicians tracking cancer patients and for the teams running cancer conferences, and what I would have done differently: four interactive redesigns and one system-level fix.

Data Visualization

Keela Contacts: A Record That Says What to Do Next

The contact record a fundraiser opens before every call, redesigned to show what changed and what to do about it. Includes a working version of the design.

Design Operations

Forkable: Making the Case for Systems Before Anyone Asked

A startup's design files had traveled through Photoshop, Sketch, and InVision, accumulating debt the product team didn't know it had. I migrated everything to Figma and built a foundation that paid for itself within months.

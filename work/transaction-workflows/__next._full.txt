<p>Every one of those payments also had to produce a correct tax receipt in three countries, and each draws the line differently:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Canada.</strong> When a donor gets something back, the receipt shows the total paid, the value of that advantage, and the eligible amount left over.</li>
<li><strong>The US.</strong> A payment over $75 that is partly a gift needs a written disclosure with a good-faith estimate of what the donor received.</li>
<li><strong>Australia.</strong> A payment at a fundraising event is deductible only if the benefit is minor: worth no more than 20% of the payment or $150, whichever is less.</li>
